# Scoring Model

Overall evidence coverage score is a weighted average:

- Evidence quantity: 15%
- Segment representation: 30%
- Source diversity: 15%
- Recency: 10%
- Target segment coverage: 15%
- Churned user inclusion: 7.5%
- Accessibility/language inclusion: 7.5%

Labels:

- 80-100: High
- 60-79: Medium
- 40-59: Low
- Below 40: Insufficient Evidence

Revenue weighting can inform business priority but does not override representation quality.
