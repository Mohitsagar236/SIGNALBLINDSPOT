# Case Study

SignalBlindspot demonstrates product discovery, evidence-based prioritization, segmentation, product analytics, bias detection, responsible product thinking, and research planning.

Portfolio positioning:

Designed and built SignalBlindspot, a product discovery platform that detects biased roadmap evidence by comparing feedback and interview coverage against the actual user population, helping Product Managers identify underrepresented segments before prioritization.

The core demo shows that Admin Dashboard demand looks strong by volume, but the evidence is heavily skewed toward enterprise admins while operators are the majority population.
